How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

Set up once on both phones. Limits are enforced on the child's device, so they keep working with the data switched off.

1

You set the prices

Pick the apps that get controlled, give each a daily free allowance, and set what extra minutes cost. Defaults are suggested by your child's age.

2

They earn the coins

Lessons, steps, puzzles, reading, chores. Some tasks are checked automatically; the rest come to you as a photo to approve.

3

The app unlocks

When the allowance runs out the app stops opening and shows the price. They pay, it opens instantly - no message to your phone required.

  • Apps lock the moment the allowance runs out.

Questions parents actually ask.

Can my child get around it?

A determined child can, and we will not tell you otherwise - safe mode, a second user profile or a factory reset defeat every app in this category, ours included. What we do is notice. Each bypass raises an alert on your phone, usually within minutes, and the deterrent is that your child knows you will see it.

Does it work if I have an iPhone and my child has an Android?

Yes - that mixed household is the normal case here, and it is something neither Apple nor Google will ever give you. The parent app ships on both platforms at launch.

What if the internet is off?

Limits are enforced on your child's own device, so turning off mobile data unlocks nothing. It only stops them buying more time.

Which ages, languages and phones?

Ages 6-14, with a lighter mode for 15-16. Uzbek, Russian and English, switchable in the app. Android 8 and above, iOS 17.4 and above.

Do you sell my child's data?

No. We never process biometric data on our servers and never read device hardware identifiers. Face ID stays on your phone. Our database is registered with the Personalization Agency before launch.

Won't the AI tutor just do the homework?

It is built so it cannot. The tutor asks scaffolded questions and a validator rejects any finished answer before it reaches the child - the deliberate opposite of the chatbot-as-shortcut pattern.
