Help with setting up and using Safini.

English Русский

Contact us

We can help you connect devices, set up tasks and limits, or troubleshoot Safini Pro. Email safini.team@gmail.com with your phone model, Safini version and a short description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or pairing codes.

Connect your family

Install Safini on the parent phone and the child’s device. Sign in as a parent, add your child and follow the connection steps in the app. Grant the required permissions on the child’s device.

Tasks and rewards

Create a task and choose a Time Coin reward. After completion is approved, your child can spend earned coins on extra time in selected apps or save for family rewards. Coins are virtual rewards, not money.

Fini and screen time

Choose a goal and the actions Fini may take. Parents can review suggestions, check activity and pause Fini. Gradual screen-time plans currently require an Android child device. The parent can still use an iPhone.

Safini Pro subscriptions

Manage subscriptions bought through the App Store in your Apple account settings. Manage subscriptions bought on the website through Your account. If you need help, email us and mention where you subscribed. Please do not send payment card details.

App language

English, Russian, Uzbek, Kyrgyz and Kazakh are available in Safini’s language settings. Switch this support page’s language using the links above.
